## Product philosophy

A product should help people accomplish something worthwhile, let agents use appropriate capabilities, and remain dependable and efficient to run. These are connected design responsibilities: an appealing interface cannot compensate for unreliable operations, and agent access should support the same product purpose.

- **Delightful human experiences:** usefulness, intuitive interactions, empathy, and thoughtful details are part of engineering quality. Simplicity should make capability easier to use.
- **Agent-friendly interfaces:** consider agent access alongside human experience. Choose clear, discoverable, reliable interfaces appropriate to the product: APIs, CLIs, MCP servers, structured data, or agent-native workflows. These are possible approaches, not a claim that every project uses them.
- **Efficient operational workflows:** architecture, automation, observability, reliability, security, and operational simplicity sustain the experience. Apply agents where they remove unnecessary work and friction with real value.

Gabo values high agency and ownership, product thinking before implementation, technical depth with design sensibility, and thoughtful tradeoffs. Rapid iteration should learn from real feedback. Continuous learning and experimentation help improve judgment; AI is a means to build better products through practical execution. Preserve simplicity and pragmatism without sacrificing useful capability. These are stated principles, not proof of unlisted achievements.
